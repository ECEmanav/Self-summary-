About Me

I started in Electronics and Communication Engineering, spent time in sales operations in India, and then moved to Munich to study how data and AI shape society and how they should be governed.

My studies cover AI ethics, the philosophy of AI, machine learning for social good, GDPR, and the EU AI Act. I put this into practice in team projects: red-teaming an AI assistant for hallucination and bias, building compliance features, and leading a digital inclusion project with the City of Munich.

My thesis looks at personal data (PII) in publicly available datasets and the ethics of web scraping. I also work hands-on with AI evaluation and data annotation, so I see how models are tested and where they fail.

I want to help teams build AI that works for the people who use it, and that can be explained, tested, and defended.
